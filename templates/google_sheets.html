How It Works
1. Edit in Google Sheets

Team members collaborate and edit data directly in Google Sheets. Comments, suggestions, and real-time editing make collaboration easy.

2. Sync Changes

When you're ready, sync the Google Sheet with the versioning system. The system detects all changes made since the last sync.

3. Create Versions

Changes are automatically committed as new versions with detailed reports of what changed, keeping a complete history of your data.
